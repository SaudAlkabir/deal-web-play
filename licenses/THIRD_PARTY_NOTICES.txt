Bluff & Deal (Deal Web) bundles these third-party components:

- Godot Engine (MIT): https://godotengine.org/license
- Tajawal font, SIL Open Font License 1.1: Tajawal-OFL.txt
- Noto Sans Symbols 2 font, SIL Open Font License 1.1: NotoSansSymbols2-OFL.txt
- Poing Studios Godot AdMob plugin scripts (MIT, inactive in the browser build): godot-admob-MIT.txt

All game audio is original procedural audio made for this project.
